How it works

  • Pipe

    A 3" ABS or PVC pipe is strapped upright to a wall or post. Holes near the bottom let floodwater in and keep waves and debris out.

  • Float

    A 500–700 mL bottle, at most 70 mm wide, with a spoon of sand so it hangs upright.

  • Pulley

    A printed sprocket with 30 ball pockets. Wet chain can't slip. One turn moves 180 mm of chain.

  • Counter­weight

    Sand in a capped 1" pipe, hanging outside the pipe. It keeps the chain tight so the float follows the water down again.

  • Sensor

    A diametric magnet in the axle end and an AS5600 behind a 1.2 mm wall. That gives 4096 counts per turn, about 0.04 mm per count.

Getting the balance right

The float has to sink in air and rise in water:

float weight > counterweight > float weight − buoyancy

The float has to be the heavier side. Put about 120 g of sand in the bottle so it weighs about 150 g, and use a 100 g counterweight. In a dry pipe the float wins by 50 g and sits on the bottom. When water arrives it only has to supply that 50 g of lift, which takes about 15 mm of submersion in a 66 mm bottle. From then on the float rides the water surface, and the counterweight keeps the chain tight in both directions.

Reading the level

level_mm = (turns × 4096 + raw) / 4096 × 180 − zero

Sample at least twice a second so the pulley never moves more than half a turn between reads. Save turns to flash so a power cut doesn't lose it.

Long cable to an indoor ESP32

The ESP32 can live in the house or a shed. I2C, the sensor's default interface, is unreliable past about 1 m, so use one of these over outdoor Cat5e/Cat6:

Recommended

PWM output

Set the AS5600 OUT pin to PWM (CONF.OUTS = 10). Use three wires: 3.3 V, GND and OUT on a twisted pair. The ESP32 times the roughly 920 Hz duty cycle on any GPIO. This works for 10–30 m.

Alternative

Differential I2C

Put a PCA9615 board at each end. You keep full I2C access, including the magnet-strength warnings, and ESPHome's as5600 component works unchanged.

Siting

  • Put it in the lowest spot where water collects, against the house or a fence post. Low black pipe gets ignored.
  • Stand the pipe on a brick or drill plenty of holes in its bottom 10 cm so silt can't seal it.
  • The counterweight drops as water rises, so keep the space under it clear.
  • Measure your ball chain pitch and update chain_pitch in cad/flood_head.py before printing.
